Caltrans Mountain Travel Notice

Sierra Winter Driving Advisory

Winter weather in the High Sierra can shift rapidly. Before ascending either mountain corridor, please ensure your vehicle is winter-ready.

Primary Corridors

Donner Pass (I-80) & Echo Summit (US-50) actively enforce Caltrans R1/R2 chain controls during snowfalls.

Mandatory Traction Requirement

Travel in an AWD / 4WD vehicle with snow-rated tires (M+S), and legally carry properly sized tire chains/cables in the trunk.

Cabin Driveway Tip

Pack a windshield ice scraper, warm gloves, and coordinate carpools below so we bring only our best-equipped winter SUVs up the hill.
